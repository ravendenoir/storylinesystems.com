Refund Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

7
DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE

We stand behind every product we ship. If it doesn't work for you, we'll make it right.

Our Commitment

We build software we use ourselves. If a Storyline Systems product doesn't work as described, crashes on your machine, or simply isn't the right fit, we want to hear from you. We handle every refund request personally and fairly.

Eligible Refunds

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your original purchase date
  • The software does not function as described on our product page
  • You experience a technical issue we are unable to resolve
  • You purchased the wrong product by mistake

How to Request a Refund

Email us at help@storylinesystems.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

  • Your order number or the email address used at checkout
  • The product you purchased
  • The reason for your refund request

We respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed through Lemon Squeezy and may take 5–10 business days to appear on your statement depending on your payment method.

Non-Refundable Situations

We are generally unable to offer refunds in the following situations:

  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase
  • You changed your mind after downloading and using the software extensively
  • Incompatibility with a system configuration not listed in our minimum requirements
  • Issues caused by third-party software conflicts on your device

That said, we review every request individually. If you have a situation not covered here, reach out — we'd rather talk it through than leave you with a product that doesn't serve you.

Technical Issues

Before requesting a refund for a technical issue, we ask that you contact us so we can attempt to resolve it. Most issues can be fixed quickly and we want to make sure you get the full value of what you purchased. Email help@storylinesystems.com and we'll work with you directly.
