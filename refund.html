Our Commitment

We build software we use ourselves. If a Storyline Systems product does not work as described, crashes on your machine, or simply is not the right fit, we want to hear from you. We handle every refund request personally and fairly.

Eligible Refunds

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • You request a refund within 7 days of your original purchase date
  • The software does not function as described on our product page
  • You experience a technical issue we are unable to resolve
  • You purchased the wrong product by mistake

How to Request a Refund

Email us at help@storylinesystems.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include your order number, the product purchased, and the reason for your request. We respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds may take 5-10 business days to appear on your statement.

Non-Refundable Situations

We are generally unable to offer refunds for requests made more than 7 days after purchase, or for incompatibility with system configurations not listed in our minimum requirements. That said, we review every request individually.

Technical Issues

Before requesting a refund for a technical issue, please contact us so we can attempt to resolve it. Email help@storylinesystems.com and we will work with you directly.
