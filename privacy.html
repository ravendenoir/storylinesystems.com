Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

The short version: our desktop software collects nothing. Your data stays on your device, period. This policy covers what we do collect on our website and through the purchase process.

🔒 Local-First Software — Your data never leaves your device

1. Our Software & Your Data

Storyline Systems products are local-first desktop applications. This means all data you enter into our software — book titles, release dates, series information, manuscript notes, and any other content — is stored exclusively on your own device. We have no servers, no cloud sync, no database that receives your creative or business data. We cannot access it, and we do not want to.

When you uninstall our software, your data remains on your device in its local storage location. We never collect, transmit, or store any of your application data.

2. Information We Collect on This Website

Our website (storylinesystems.com) is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit our site, standard web server logs may be generated by GitHub's infrastructure. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics tools, or any third-party tracking scripts on our website.

We do not collect:

  • Your name or email address through website browsing
  • Your IP address for marketing purposes
  • Behavioral or analytics data about how you use our website

3. Information Collected During Purchase

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. When you complete a purchase, Lemon Squeezy collects the payment and billing information necessary to process your transaction. This includes your name, email address, and payment details. Lemon Squeezy's handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policy.

Storyline Systems receives from Lemon Squeezy only what is necessary to fulfill your order: your email address and the product purchased. We use this solely to deliver your license and provide customer support.

4. Email Communications

If you contact us at help@storylinesystems.com or any Storyline Systems email address, we retain that correspondence to provide support. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent. We do not sell, share, or rent your email address to any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website and business operations involve the following third parties, each with their own privacy practices:

  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and license delivery
  • Namecheap — domain registration

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising tracking technology.

6. Children's Privacy

Our software and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at help@storylinesystems.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Security

Because our software stores no data on our servers, the security of your application data is entirely in your control. For the limited personal information we do receive through the purchase process, we use industry-standard practices to protect it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. Given the minimal data we collect, this typically means your purchase email and any support correspondence. Contact us at help@storylinesystems.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of our software or website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
