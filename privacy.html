Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 16, 2026

The short version: our desktop software collects nothing. Your data stays on your device. This policy covers what we do collect on our website and through the purchase process.

Local-First Software — Your data never leaves your device

1. Our Software and Your Data

Storyline Systems products are local-first desktop applications. All data you enter — book titles, release dates, series information, manuscript notes — is stored exclusively on your own device. We have no servers that receive your creative or business data. We cannot access it, and we do not want to.

2. Information We Collect on This Website

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics tools, or any third-party tracking scripts. We do not collect your name, email address, or IP address through website browsing.

3. Information Collected During Purchase

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects the payment and billing information necessary to process your transaction. Storyline Systems receives only your email address and product purchased — used solely to deliver your license and provide customer support.

4. Email Communications

If you contact us at help@storylinesystems.com, we retain that correspondence to provide support. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent. We do not sell or share your email address with any third party.

5. Third-Party Services

Our business involves these third parties, each with their own privacy practices: GitHub Pages (website hosting), Lemon Squeezy (payment processing), and Namecheap (domain registration). We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any advertising tracking technology.

6. Children's Privacy

Our software and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at help@storylinesystems.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Data Security

Because our software stores no data on our servers, the security of your application data is entirely in your control. For the limited personal information we receive through purchases, we use industry-standard practices to protect it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold. Contact us at help@storylinesystems.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will post the updated date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: help@storylinesystems.com
